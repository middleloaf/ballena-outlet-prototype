Frequently asked questions

Before you come, let's clear things up.

The questions we get most on WhatsApp. If something's still unclear, message us and we'll reply directly.

Where do the products come from?
We buy pallets of returns, overstock, and unclaimed packages from major U.S. retailers such as Amazon, Target, Costco, and Walmart. They arrive at our store in Uvita and are sold by the piece.
Can I buy online?
The store is physical. You can check availability on WhatsApp, but purchase and pickup happen at the shop. Shipping is coordinated at the store; there is no online checkout.
Are the products new?
These are liquidation products: returns, overstock, or unclaimed packages. Condition varies, which is why you can inspect each item before buying. If you have a specific question, ask us on WhatsApp.
How do I know what's available?
Message us on WhatsApp at 6313 2925 and ask about the category you're interested in. We also post updates on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
Where is the store?
At Plaza La Perla del Pacífico, next to Servicentro Ballena, upstairs from Farmacia Ballena, Uvita, Costa Rica. You can open the location in Google Maps.
Do you ship?
Yes, but shipping is coordinated at the store. Pickup is always at our Uvita shop. Ask about shipping options when you buy.
